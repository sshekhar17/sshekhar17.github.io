The transmitter combines the channels; the synchronized receiver separates them using two orthogonal local oscillators.

Things to try and observe

Start with Try two-tone experiment for clear spectral peaks.

  • Two signals, one band: Observe that both modulated contributions lie around ±ωc, even though their original spectra differ.
  • Perfect synchronization: Set φ = 0° and noise = 0%. Listen to each recovered channel; each contains its own audio.
  • Cross-talk: Increase φ to 45°, then 90°. Each receiver starts hearing the other channel; at 90° the outputs exchange (one is negated).
  • Noise and filtering: Raise the RF noise level and vary the LPF cutoff. Listen for distortion and noise that survives the filter.
