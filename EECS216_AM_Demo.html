What happens to the spectrum?

y(t) = x(t) cos(ωct)
Creates two spectral copies centered at ±ωc in the angular-frequency spectrum.

What will you discover?

x̂(t) ≈ x(t) cos(φ)
After demodulation and low-pass filtering, a phase error attenuates the desired signal.

Things to try and observe

For clean spectral peaks, begin with Two tones.

  • Modulation: Adjust ωc and watch the baseband spectrum shift to ±ωc.
  • Demodulation: Select Mixer output, then ±120 kHz to see images at ±2ωc; the LPF removes them.
  • Phase error: Increase φ toward 90°. The desired recovered amplitude falls as cos(φ), reaching zero without noise.
  • Noise: Turn up channel noise and listen. The LPF cannot remove noise overlapping the audio band.
